# Where to find it in Lucent

Nederlands: [INSTELLINGEN.md](INSTELLINGEN.md)

Every setting of the Lucent skin (version 2.1.36) with the path to it. Use your browser's search (Ctrl+F) to find a word.

**How to get there**
- **Settings**: on Home, pick the gear **Settings** in the side menu. Sections on the left, rows on the right.
- **Skin settings**: Settings › **Lucent** › **Skin settings**.
- *(sometimes)* = only shows up when it applies (for example only with Lucent Video or IPTV).
- Indented rows are inside a fold-out block: press the row above them first.

## Settings

### Settings › Lucent
_Look, menu, streaming services, kids mode and the setup assistant_

- **Skin settings** – Theme, layout, info page, integrations and more
- **Change skin** – Choose another installed skin, such as Estuary
- **Customise menu and widgets** – Tabs of the side menu and the rows on Home
- **Streaming services** *(sometimes)* – Which services the 'Streaming services' row shows, their order and their pictures
- **Power menu** – Hide Kodi's own buttons, or add your own (Settings, an add-on, Reload skin…)
- **Light mode (on/off)** *(sometimes)* – On: Home builds only 6 rows and uses small pictures (for boxes with 2 GB). Off: up to 10 rows
- **Kids mode** – Only the menu items you choose, settings behind a PIN
- **Setup assistant** – The most important settings and logins, step by step

### Settings › Watching
_Playback, subtitles, language and skipping intros_

- **Videos** – Video playback, refresh rate, zoom and stretch
- **Subtitles** – Font, size, colour and position of subtitles
- **Lucent Subtitles** *(sometimes)* – Accounts, keys and how subtitles are found
- **Language** – Preferred audio and subtitle language
- **Close the player menu by itself**
- **Buttons in the player bar**
- **Order of the buttons**
- **Trailer on focus**
- **Subtitles and skip intro** *(sometimes)* – In your video add-on: subtitle search, spoken language, AI translation and skip intro

### Settings › Live TV
_Your IPTV channels, guide, recordings and parental control_

- **Xtream login (IPTV)** – Fill in your provider's server address, user name and password; Lucent sets up IPTV Simple
- **Hide channels** – Leave out groups and channels you never watch (shorter list, faster guide)
- **Clean channel logos** *(sometimes)* – Logos from tv-logos in the guide, the TV tab and the player (on or off)
- **Favourite channel group** *(sometimes)* – Opens first in the guide and shows on the TV tab
- **Goal notifications (on/off)** *(sometimes)* – A pop-up when a team you follow scores (select to switch on / off)
- **General** – Channel manager, groups and channel numbers
- **Guide** – Guide: days to show, update interval
- **Playback** – Playback: switch channels, start at the last channel
- **Recording** – Recordings: margins and file names
- **Parental control** – Lock channels with a PIN code
- **PVR clients** – The PVR add-ons (IPTV Simple Client)

### Settings › System
_Screen, sound, remote, network, language and screensaver_

- **Display** – Resolution, refresh rate and calibration
- **Calibrate the picture** – Fix black edges or a picture that falls off the screen
- **Audio** – Audio output, passthrough and channels
- **Input** – Remote, keyboard, mouse and peripherals
- **Internet access** – Proxy and internet speed
- **Control** – Control from a phone app or web browser
- **Regional** – Language, keyboard, time, date and units
- **Screensaver** – Screensaver and when it starts
- **Startup** – Start-up window and what Kodi shows first
- **Skin** – Choose the skin, zoom, sounds and RSS
- **Master lock** – Master lock with a code
- **Power saving** – Shut down when idle, wake-on-LAN
- **Logging** – Debug logging for problems
- **CoreELEC settings** *(sometimes)* – Network, Bluetooth, updates and system of this box
- **LibreELEC settings** *(sometimes)* – Network, Bluetooth, updates and system of this box
- **More Kodi settings** – Music, discs, pictures, services and games

### Settings › Add-ons
_Install, update and manage add-ons_

- **My add-ons** – All add-ons you have installed
- **Search add-ons** – Find an add-on by name
- **Install from repository** – Browse the repositories you added
- **Install from zip file** – Install an add-on or repository from a zip
- **Available updates** – Add-ons with a newer version
- **Add-on settings** – Unknown sources, automatic updates

### Settings › Files and profiles
_Your files, sources, profiles, kids mode and backup_

- **File manager** – Copy, move and delete files
- **Profiles** – Profiles for every person in the house
- **Backup and restore** – Save all your settings and logins in one file, and put them back
- **Library** – Update the library, show watched and extra art
- **Videos** – Video library: titles, flags, actors and trailers
- **General** – File lists: hide extensions, sort, delete and rename

### Settings › Help and info
_What's new, updates, self-test and system information_

- **About Lucent** – Logo, version, update options and licences
- **What's new** – The changes of every version
- **Check for updates now** – Look for a new version of Lucent
- **Update pop-up (on/off)** *(sometimes)* – Pop-up when a new version is available
- **System information** – Kodi version, CPU, memory, storage and network
- **Event log** – What Kodi did recently, with errors
- **Self-test** – Checks your video add-on, IPTV, subtitles and the channel list and tells you what is missing
- **Safe log** – Copies Kodi's log with all logins blanked out, to share
- **YouTube trailer fix** *(sometimes)* – Keeps trailers from stopping after about a minute (bug in YouTube 7.4)
- **Licences and credits** – Libraries, fonts and artwork that Lucent uses

## Skin settings

### Skin settings › Appearance

**Theme**

- **Colour theme** – Accent colour of the focus, buttons and glow
- **AMOLED mode** – Pure black surfaces, saves power on OLED screens
- **Interface scale** – Make everything a bit larger or smaller
- **Font and language** – Language, region and keyboard of Kodi
- **Show clock** – Clock at the top right of every screen
- **Colour from the poster** – The background glows in the colour of the poster you are on
- **Intro at start** – The star and the moon of Lucent when Kodi starts (about 6 seconds)
- **Sound with the intro** – A warm sound with the intro when Kodi starts
- **Seasons** – Halloween, Sinterklaas, Christmas with snow, New Year and King's Day on Home
- **Readable text on light pictures** – A dark edge around the texts and a darker fade behind them

**Screensaver**

- **Dim when idle** – Darken the screen when you do nothing for a while
- **Idle timeout** – How long before the screen dims
- **Dim strength** – How dark the screen gets

### Skin settings › Layout
- **Home** – Rows, posters and what you see on Home
  - **Show poster labels** – Title under every poster
  - **Big posters on Home** – On: posters 15% bigger, without names. Off: the old, smaller posters, with the next row peeking in
  - **Landscape posters** – Wide cards instead of posters on Home
  - **Watched titles** – Show, dim or hide titles you have seen
  - **Hide side icons** – No icons next to the closed side menu
  - **Show full release date** – Day and month as well as the year
  - **Fullscreen hero backdrop** – Enlarge the hero background so it fills the screen
  - **Sport on the home screen** – A card with today's game of a team you follow, OK to watch
  - **Tonight for you** – A row on Home with titles like what you watched last, fitting the time of day
  - **Load rows as you go** – Only the top three rows load at start, the rest when you scroll down
- **Poster focus** – Trailer on a poster that has the focus
  - **Trailer on focus** – Play a trailer when a poster keeps the focus
  - **Start trailer after** – How long to wait before the trailer starts
  - **YouTube add-on settings** – Quality and login of the YouTube add-on
- **Cards** – Look of the cards
  - **Edge glow** – Glow around the card that has the focus
- **Details page** – Ratings, buttons and what OK does
  - **Show ratings** – IMDb, TMDB and Trakt scores on the info page
  - **Rating logos** – Your own logo images instead of the names
  - **OK on a film opens its page** – Off: the stream starts at once
  - **OK on an episode opens its page** – Off: the stream starts at once
  - **Series: seasons as tabs** – Opening a series shows the seasons as tabs above the episodes
  - **Show trailer button** – Trailer button on the info page
  - **Install studio logos** – Logo pack for the studios on the info page
- **Lists and posters** – Media flags, percentage watched, views and the bar above lists
  - **Media flags in the lists** – 4K, HDR, codec, sound and languages of the title under the cursor
  - **Percentage watched on posters** – A small label like 45% above the progress bar of what you are watching
  - **Remember view per kind** – The view you pick for films, series, episodes ... is used in every folder of that kind
  - **Episodes in landscape rows** – Picture of the episode (a still from the episode)
  - **Name and count above lists** – Name and count above a list, e.g. 'Red Light · 1647 items · Date'
  - **Up hint in one-row views** – Shows 'Up: View · Sort · Filter' next to the clock; Up keeps working when it is off
  - **Slider only when scrolling fast** – The slider under a one-row view only shows while you hold a key down; Down still goes to it

### Skin settings › Menu and widgets

**Menu, rows and services**

- **Customise menu and widgets** – Tabs of the side menu and the rows on Home
- **Streaming services** – Which services the 'Streaming services' row shows, their order and their pictures
- **Power menu** – Hide Kodi's own buttons, or add your own (Settings, an add-on, Reload skin…)
- **Restore the default menu** – Put the tabs and rows back the way they were

### Skin settings › Search

**Search**

- **Search in by default** – The add-on the search screen starts with; OK on the add-on button there goes to the next one
- **Search tab opens** – What OK on the Search tab of the menu opens: the Lucent search screen, or the search menu of Lucent Video

**What is searched**

- **Movies** – A row with movies found in the add-on
- **Shows** – A row with shows found in the add-on
- **Actors** – A row with actors and actresses; OK opens their page (needs a TMDb key)
- **Kodi library** – Also a row with what you have in the Kodi library

### Skin settings › Playback

**Playback**

- **Kodi player settings** – Video, sound and refresh rate of Kodi's player
- **Player settings of the video add-on** – Source select, autoplay, Up Next and the playback watchdog
- **Subtitles and audio language** – Preferred language for audio and subtitles

**Pause screen**

- **Show info after** – When the info appears after you pause
- **Picture and sound info** – Resolution, HDR and audio badges on the pause screen
- **Your own picture and sound logos** – Use your own badge images

### Skin settings › Integrations
- **Video add-ons and TMDB** – Video add-ons, services and TMDB
- **Video add-on settings** – Trakt, accounts and sources
  - **Add-ons** – Install and manage add-ons
  - **Services** – Remote control and sharing
  - **TMDB API key** – For cast, collections, studios and the pages opened from them
  - **Scores (OMDb / MDBList)** – IMDb, Rotten Tomatoes, Metacritic and Letterboxd with your own free key
- **Subtitles** – Search subtitles from the player
  - **Lucent Subtitles: accounts and keys** – Accounts and keys (OpenSubtitles, SubDL), sources
  - **Subtitles and skip intro** – In your video add-on: subtitle search, spoken language, AI translation and skip intro

### Skin settings › Tracking

**Tracking**

- **Trakt login**

**Calendar**

- **Pop-up when a new episode is out** – Notification when a followed series has a new episode
- **Films on my watchlist** – Pop-up when a film from your Trakt collection or watchlist comes out digitally, with where to watch it
- **Open the calendar** – Week, list and month view

### Skin settings › Profiles
- **Profiles** – Photo, own colour theme and Who is watching? at start (switch on Login screen)

### Skin settings › Advanced

**Screen**

- **Calibrate the picture** – Fix black edges or a picture that is too large
- **Display settings** – Resolution and refresh rate
- **Light mode (2 GB boxes)** – At most 6 rows per tab, small artwork, no snow and no poster colour. The skin reloads once

**Advanced**

- **System information** – Version, processor, memory and storage
- **Event log** – What Kodi did recently
- **Maintenance** – Free up space: old pictures, add-on zip files and temporary files; also once a month by itself
- **Reload skin** – Reload Lucent without restarting Kodi
- **Restore all skin settings** – All Lucent settings back to their defaults
- **Skin debug** – Shows the names of windows and controls
