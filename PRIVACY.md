# Privacy – Lucent

Lucent (the Kodi skin and its add-ons, including Lucent Video) runs entirely on your own device. There is no Lucent server.

- **No data collection.** Lucent does not collect, store or send personal data to the maker or to anyone else.
- **Trakt.** When you link Trakt, the sign-in token is stored only on your device, in Kodi's add-on data folder. Lucent uses it to read your lists, collection, watchlist and watch history, and to send what you watch (scrobbling, history, watchlist changes) to your own Trakt account. You can unlink at any time in the add-on settings, or revoke access on trakt.tv under Settings → Connected Apps.
- **Other services** you choose to use (TMDB, Simkl, MDBList, debrid services and others) are contacted directly from your device with your own keys. Their own privacy policies apply.
- **Error reports** are only made when you press the button yourself. Addresses, keys and passwords are removed from them, and you decide where to share them.
